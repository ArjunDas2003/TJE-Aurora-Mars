Dossier & Inquiries

ZÜRICH LAB AUDIT

Traditional matte lipsticks rely on heavy clay binders and drying powders that sap moisture from the vermilion zone. The Aurora Mars lipstick utilizes a bio-compatible lipid vector of cold-pressed Japanese Camellia seed oil and bio-fermented hyaluronic spheres that continuously hydrate lips for up to 12 hours while maintaining a dead-matte optical finish.

Yes. Aurora operates under strict Swiss and EU cosmetic standards. No carmine (crushed cochineal insects) is used in our red pigments; our deep crimson reds and plum berries are achieved exclusively using triple-milled synthetic and mineral oxides. Zero animal testing is performed at any phase.

To guarantee bullet integrity and prevent thermal melting during transit, every Mars order is packed in insulated recyclable thermal sleeves maintained between 16°C and 20°C from our Zürich laboratory to your door. All international shipments include priority customs clearance.

The casing is CNC-machined from single-billet aerospace-grade aluminum, electro-anodized in Aurora Maroon `#641C32`. A calibrated neodymium magnetic lock ensures the cap closes with a satisfying, authoritative snap rated for over 10,000 cycles.
